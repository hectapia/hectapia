# HECTOR OLIVARES TAPIA

## Professional File

### Professional Profile

Mechanical and Electrical Engineer (UJAT) | Associate of Applied Science in Software Development (BYU-Idaho) | Project Coordinator | Executive Consultant | Specialist in Contract Management and Digitization

With nearly three decades of operational experience in strategic facilities of Petróleos Mexicanos (PEMEX / COMIMSA) in mechanical integrity, reliability, inspection, and industrial maintenance, combined with a solid international university education in software development and web systems from Brigham Young University-Idaho, I bring a unique interdisciplinary perspective to contract administration and technical leadership.

This dual view—technical and safety rigor in the field and data governance, process automation, and real-time budget control—allows me to lead large-scale contracts with a strong focus on accountability, contract compliance, and operational profitability. Through the design, architecture, and implementation of the Contract Manager ecosystem, I have transformed conventional contract management into a systematic methodology that ensures segregation of duties, end-to-end auditable traceability, elimination of discrepancies in estimates, and immediate fiscal and financial reconciliation.

I am committed to translating contractual and regulatory requirements into automated control, analytics, and compliance flows. I believe that what is measured can be improved, and what is measured and reported accelerates its rate of improvement.

> "When performance is measured, it improves. When performance is measured and reported, the rate of improvement accelerates."  
> — Thomas S. Monson
